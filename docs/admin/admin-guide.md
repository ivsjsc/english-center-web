## 1. Daily Admissions & CRM Workflow

### Step 1: Check Inbound Leads
- Access `/admin/leads`.
- Sort by newest or filter by status `NEW`.
- Each lead card displays student name, age, phone, interested course, and campus preference.

### Step 2: Make Contact & Assign Responsibility
- Click on the phone link to dial directly.
- Click **+ Phân công** to assign the lead to your account or a colleague.
- Once the initial phone call is complete, click on the status badge and change to `CONTACTED`.
- Enter notes in the dialogue box (e.g., *"Đã gọi phụ huynh lúc 09:30, phụ huynh quan tâm lớp mầm non SmartKids"*).

### Step 3: Schedule Placement Test or Appointment
- When the parent agrees to visit, update status to `APPOINTMENT` or `PLACEMENT_TEST`.
- On completion of enrollment and tuition fee payment, update status to `ENROLLED`.
- If the student has no current need, mark as `LOST` with a reason note.

## 2. Managing Educational Content (CMS)

### Courses (`/admin/courses`)
- View active courses, student age brackets, duration, and curriculum outlines.

### Centers (`/admin/centers`)
- Manage campus addresses, hotlines, opening hours, facilities checklist, and Google Maps direct links.

### Blog & Guides (`/admin/blog`)
- Publish new articles, manage categories (`IELTS`, `Cambridge`, `Workplace English`), and preview SEO metadata.
