# BUSINESS-CONTEXT.md

## Brand architecture

**IVS JSC** is the parent ecosystem. **IVS Academy** is the education-facing brand and this website is primarily a **foreign-language center/admissions experience**.

### Primary experience — Foreign Language Center

- Courses by age/goal
- Placement testing
- Teachers
- Centers
- Student achievements
- Learning methodology
- Blog/resources
- Consultation/admissions

### Secondary experience — Education Solutions

- School education partnerships
- International teacher services / TeacherMatch
- Curriculum and R&D
- IVS LifeMinds
- Center establishment/operation consulting

### Ecosystem bridges

- IVS Learn
- CenterCare
- IVS Tech
- IVS Global Teacher Hub
- IVS Games / Apps when relevant

Technology services such as hosting, domains, general CRM, e-commerce, QR verification and unrelated digital-transformation offerings belong primarily on IVS Tech and should not crowd the Academy main navigation.

## Navigation principle

Main public navigation must remain learner/parent-first. Ecosystem links may appear in a slim utility bar, footer, an “IVS Ecosystem” section/page, or contextual product bridges.

## Data integrity

Never publish generated/demo claims as facts. Verify company legal information, center locations, accreditation, partnerships, teacher profiles, course outcomes, metrics and testimonials before production publication.
